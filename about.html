About & Methodology

Understanding the model behind the predictions

Model Overview

Quant Model

The Quant model predicts home team win probability using Predicted Points Added (PPA) metrics—measuring offensive and defensive efficiency on a per-play basis—combined with third down conversion rates, turnover differentials, and penalty data. This moneyline-focused model captures granular play-level execution, making it particularly effective for identifying teams whose actual efficiency outpaces their historical reputation or strength of schedule.

Quant Spread Model

The Quant Spread model applies the same PPA-based feature set to predict margin of victory and spread coverage outcomes. Rather than binary win prediction, this model quantifies expected point differential, enabling analysis of betting value at different spread levels. This variant excels at identifying close games where efficiency advantages translate to points, isolating high-conviction spread opportunities.

Quant Adj Model

The Quant Adj model integrates SP+ ratings—ESPN's comprehensive team quality measure—with PPA metrics and situational statistics. Feature importance analysis reveals SP+ Overall Differential as the dominant predictor, with offensive/defensive efficiency and situational factors providing marginal refinement. This hybrid approach balances established team strength with in-season performance dynamics, reducing noise from single-game variance.

Quant Adj Spread Model

The Quant Adj Spread model extends the hybrid SP+/PPA framework to predict margin of victory and spread coverage. By combining overall team strength assessments with play-level efficiency and situational execution metrics, this model identifies point spread mispricings with high confidence. All models employ Random Forest algorithms (1,000 trees) with walk-forward cross-validation to eliminate data leakage.

Model Terminology Glossary

Rating Systems

SP+ (S&P+ Rating)

ESPN's comprehensive college football rating system that measures overall team quality independent of strength of schedule. Accounts for offensive efficiency, defensive efficiency, special teams, and margin of victory adjustments to produce a single rating for each team. Used as the dominant feature in Quant Adj models.

PPA Metrics (Predicted Points Added)

Off PPA (Offensive PPA)

Measures how many points an offense adds or subtracts on each play relative to expectation, adjusted for down, distance, and field position. Positive values indicate the offense exceeded expected performance; negative values indicate underperformance.

Def PPA (Defensive PPA)

Inverse of offensive PPA—measures points prevented per play. Positive defensive PPA indicates strong defense; negative indicates defensive weakness.

Off Rush PPA / Off Pass PPA

Splits offensive PPA by play type—rushing plays versus passing plays—to identify offensive strengths or weaknesses in specific situations.

Def Rush PPA / Def Pass PPA

Defensive PPA split by rushing and passing plays, revealing whether a defense struggles more against the run or pass.

Efficiency Metrics

Success Rate (Success%)

Percentage of plays that achieve "success"—defined as gaining 50%+ of necessary yards on early downs (1st/2nd down) or 100%+ of necessary yards on late downs (3rd/4th down). Measures offensive consistency.

Explosiveness

Frequency of big plays—defined as plays gaining 15%+ of total yards needed for a first down on early downs or 25%+ on late downs. Captures offensive ability to create sudden gains.

Betting & Prediction Terms

Expected Value (EV)

The mathematical advantage or disadvantage of a bet. Calculated as: (Probability of Win × Payout) − (Probability of Loss × Stake). Positive EV indicates a profitable bet over time; negative EV indicates an unprofitable bet.

Confidence

Model's probability estimate for a predicted outcome, expressed as a percentage. Higher confidence indicates the model is more certain in its prediction based on feature values.
